Koaryu dashboard redesign · decision pitch

One sheet in hand. The whole studio in reach.

Recommend a Mat-shaped shell with Ledger-shaped data. It gives Today a clear operating focus, lets dense records scan quickly, and keeps scope, freshness, permissions, and non-live truth visible.

Decision: Dojo Ledger owns the system. Training Floor remains a bounded temporal view for Today and Schedule. Wall behavior stops at Pin to Today.

Open the recommended prototype Audit all 27 routes Follow three workflows

Two interaction systems

Different composition, not different paint

Both concepts use calm paper, strong ink, and restrained motion. They disagree on how work is organized. Ledger treats the product as indexed records. Training Floor treats it as spatial lanes with time and a commit line.

Concept A · recommended

Dojo Ledger

Global spine, declared scope, ruled records, contextual history. Stable columns make comparison fast; current selection gets one margin rail.

Indexed spine Students · 214 active · all programs · as of 6:12am Ruled rosterSelection history State gutter + fields Batch band

Best at: roster, student file, billing books, reports, settings, account.

Concept B · bounded alternative

Training Floor

Fixed work lanes, horizontal time or urgency, and a visible commit edge. Work moves across the floor as its state changes.

WaitingOn matCommitted Lead · 3dTrial tonightConverted CSV readMap 18 fieldsLand roster Class dueMark attendanceClose sheet

Best at: time-based work, attendance, import progression, lead aging.
